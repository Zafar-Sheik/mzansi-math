# CAPS curriculum map

MathsMzansi covers the **Algebra**, **Geometry** and **Trigonometry** strands of CAPS Mathematics for Grades 11 and 12. Statistics, probability (and Grade 12 counting) are examined too but are outside this app's scope.

## Grade 11 – Algebra (Phase 1 ✅)

| Topic | Lessons | CAPS content |
|---|---|---|
| Exponents & Surds | Exponent laws: the remix · Surds without the stress · Exponential & surd equations | Laws of exponents with rational exponents; simplifying, adding, multiplying and rationalising surds; exponential equations (same base, factorising, k-substitution); equations with rational exponents; surd equations |
| Equations & Inequalities | Quadratic equations: three ways in · The discriminant · Quadratic inequalities · Simultaneous equations | Factorising, completing the square, quadratic formula; nature of roots; quadratic inequalities; one linear and one quadratic equation simultaneously |
| Number Patterns | Quadratic number patterns · Patterns in the wild | Constant second difference, Tₙ = an² + bn + c, problems in context |
| Functions & Graphs | Parabolas · Hyperbolas · Exponential graphs · Average gradient & reading graphs | Effects of a, p, q on y = a(x + p)² + q, y = a/(x + p) + q, y = a·bˣ⁺ᵖ + q; sketching; domain & range; asymptotes; axes of symmetry; finding equations; average gradient |
| Finance, Growth & Decay | Simple vs compound · Depreciation, inflation & population · Nominal vs effective & timelines | A = P(1 ± in), A = P(1 ± i)ⁿ; finding P and i; nominal and effective rates; timelines with changing rates, deposits and withdrawals |

> Grade 11 trigonometric graphs live in the Trigonometry topic (Phase 2), not in Functions & Graphs.

## Grade 11 – Geometry & Trigonometry (Phase 2)

| Topic | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Analytical Geometry | Equation of a line through two points / given gradient and point; inclination θ (tan θ = m); parallel and perpendicular lines |
| Euclidean Geometry | Circle theorems (centre ⟂ chord, angle at centre, angles in the same segment, cyclic quadrilaterals, tan–chord); proofs and riders |
| Measurement | Surface area and volume of right prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, spheres and combinations; effect of multiplying dimensions by a factor k |
| Trigonometry | Identities (tan θ = sin θ / cos θ, sin² θ + cos² θ = 1); reduction formulae and negative angles; general solutions; graphs y = k sin x, y = sin kx, y = sin(x + p) etc.; sine, cosine and area rules |

## Grade 12 – Algebra & Calculus (Phase 3)

| Topic | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Sequences & Series | Arithmetic and geometric sequences and series; sigma notation; Sₙ formulas; sum to infinity and convergence |
| Functions & Inverses | Definition of a function; inverses of linear, quadratic (restricted domain) and exponential functions; logarithms and log laws |
| Finance & Annuities | Future and present value annuities; loans and bond repayments; sinking funds; outstanding balance; finding n with logs |
| Polynomials | Remainder and factor theorems; factorising and solving cubic equations |
| Differential Calculus | Limits; derivative from first principles; rules of differentiation; equations of tangents; cubic graphs (stationary points, concavity, point of inflection); optimisation and rates of change |

## Grade 12 – Geometry & Trigonometry (Phase 4)

| Topic | CAPS content |
|---|---|
| Analytical Geometry | Equation of a circle (centre anywhere); tangents to circles |
| Euclidean Geometry | Proportionality theorem; midpoint theorem; similar triangles and their proofs; Pythagoras via similarity |
| Trigonometry | Compound and double angle identities; general solutions; 2D and 3D problems |
