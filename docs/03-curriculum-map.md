# CAPS curriculum map

MathsMzansi covers the whole of CAPS Mathematics for Grades 11 and 12 in five strands: **Algebra**, **Geometry**, **Trigonometry**, **Statistics** and **Probability** (including Grade 12 counting).

## Grade 11 – Algebra (Phase 1 ✅)

| Topic | Lessons | CAPS content |
|---|---|---|
| Exponents & Surds | Exponent laws: the remix · Surds without the stress · Exponential & surd equations | Laws of exponents with rational exponents; simplifying, adding, multiplying and rationalising surds; exponential equations (same base, factorising, k-substitution); equations with rational exponents; surd equations |
| Equations & Inequalities | Quadratic equations: three ways in · The discriminant · Quadratic inequalities · Simultaneous equations | Factorising, completing the square, quadratic formula; nature of roots; quadratic inequalities; one linear and one quadratic equation simultaneously |
| Number Patterns | Quadratic number patterns · Patterns in the wild | Constant second difference, Tₙ = an² + bn + c, problems in context |
| Functions & Graphs | Parabolas · Hyperbolas · Exponential graphs · Average gradient & reading graphs | Effects of a, p, q on y = a(x + p)² + q, y = a/(x + p) + q, y = a·bˣ⁺ᵖ + q; sketching; domain & range; asymptotes; axes of symmetry; finding equations; average gradient |
| Finance, Growth & Decay | Simple vs compound · Depreciation, inflation & population · Nominal vs effective & timelines | A = P(1 ± in), A = P(1 ± i)ⁿ; finding P and i; nominal and effective rates; timelines with changing rates, deposits and withdrawals |

> Grade 11 trigonometric graphs live in the Trigonometry topic (Phase 2), not in Functions & Graphs.

## Grade 11 – Geometry & Trigonometry (Phase 2 ✅)

| Topic | Lessons | CAPS content |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical Geometry | Distance, midpoint & gradient · Equations of lines & inclination · Parallel & perpendicular lines | Distance, midpoint and gradient; collinear points; equation of a line through two points / a point and a gradient; inclination θ (m = tan θ); parallel and perpendicular lines; perpendicular bisectors |
| Euclidean Geometry | Chords, centres & the angle at the centre · Same segment & cyclic quadrilaterals · Tangents & the tan-chord theorem | Line from centre ⟂ chord; angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference; angle in a semicircle; angles in the same segment; cyclic quadrilaterals (opposite and exterior angles, converses); tangents (tan ⊥ radius, tangents from a common point, tan-chord theorem); examinable proofs; riders |
| Measurement | Volume & surface area of solids · Combined solids & scaling | Surface area and volume of right prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, spheres and combined objects; effect of multiplying dimensions by k |
| Trigonometry | Ratios on the Cartesian plane · Reduction formulae · Identities · Equations & the general solution · Graphs · Sine, cosine & area rules | x, y, r definitions and CAST; special angles; reduction formulae (180° ± θ, 360° − θ, −θ, 90° ± θ); tan θ = sin θ / cos θ and sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; general solutions; effects of a, k, p, q on sin, cos, tan graphs; sine, cosine and area rules in 2D |

## Grade 12 – Algebra & Calculus (Phase 3 ✅)

| Topic | Lessons | CAPS content |
|---|---|---|
| Sequences & Series | Arithmetic sequences & series · Geometric sequences & series · Sigma notation & sum to infinity | Arithmetic and geometric sequences and series; sigma notation; Sₙ formulas; sum to infinity and convergence |
| Functions & Inverses | Functions & their inverses · Exponential & log functions | Definition of a function; inverses of linear, quadratic (restricted domain) and exponential functions; logarithms and log laws |
| Finance & Annuities | Future value annuities & sinking funds · Present value: loans & outstanding balances · How long? Finding n with logs | Future and present value annuities; loans and bond repayments; sinking funds; outstanding balance; finding n with logs |
| Polynomials | Remainder & factor theorems · Factorising & solving cubics | Remainder and factor theorems; factorising and solving cubic equations |
| Differential Calculus | Limits & first principles · Rules of differentiation & tangents · Cubic graphs · Optimisation · Rates of change | Limits; derivative from first principles; rules of differentiation; equations of tangents; cubic graphs (stationary points, concavity, point of inflection); optimisation and rates of change |
