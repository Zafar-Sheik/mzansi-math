# Build roadmap (phases)

MathsMzansi covers the whole CAPS Grade 11 & 12 syllabus for **Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry**. It's a big build, so it ships in phases. Each phase is fully tested and usable on its own.

## Phase 1 – Platform + Grade 11 Algebra & Functions ✅

**Platform:** lessons in steps, KaTeX maths everywhere, typed-answer checking (numbers, sets, points, expressions, surd forms), endless question generators, the SVG graph engine, flashcards with spaced repetition, Quiz Arena, Exam Room, games (Speed Solve, Graph Match, Formula Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out, Maths Hangman), XP/badges/streaks, accessibility preferences, teacher guide.

**Content – Grade 11 (16 lessons):**

- Exponents & Surds (3 lessons)
- Equations & Inequalities (4 lessons)
- Number Patterns (2 lessons)
- Functions & Graphs (4 lessons)
- Finance, Growth & Decay (3 lessons)

**Tools:** function grapher + Graph Match, quadratic lab, inequality lab, line-meets-parabola lab, pattern machine, exponent-law tiles, surd simplifier, money lab, investment timeline, sketch figures, drills.

## Phase 2 – Grade 11 Geometry & Trigonometry ✅

**Content – Grade 11 (14 lessons):**

- Analytical Geometry (3 lessons): distance, midpoint & gradient · equations of lines & inclination · parallel & perpendicular lines
- Euclidean Geometry (3 lessons): chords & the angle at the centre · same segment & cyclic quadrilaterals · tangents & the tan-chord theorem – with the examinable proofs
- Measurement (2 lessons): volume & surface area · combined solids & scaling
- Trigonometry (6 lessons): ratios & CAST · reduction formulae · identities · equations & general solutions · graphs · sine, cosine & area rules

**Tools:** coordinate lab (draggable points), circle-theorem explorer (six theorems, drag points round the circle), geometry figures for riders, solid viewer with the k²/k³ scale factor, CAST & trig-ratio explorer, trig graph lab, triangle solver.

**Practice:** 13 new question generators (including circle-angle questions with generated diagrams), so the drills and Speed Solve now cover geometry and trig. 10 new Paper 2 exam questions, including riders and a theorem proof.

**Engine:** the answer checker understands degrees (`°`), `θ` and `sin^2 x`, and checks trig expressions over a wide range of angles.

## Phase 3 – Grade 12 Algebra & Calculus ✅

**Content – Grade 12 (15 lessons):**

- Sequences & Series (3 lessons): arithmetic sequences & series · geometric sequences & series · sigma notation & sum to infinity
- Functions & Inverses (2 lessons): functions & their inverses · exponential & log functions
- Finance & Annuities (3 lessons): future value & sinking funds · present value, loans & outstanding balances · finding n with logs
- Polynomials (2 lessons): remainder & factor theorems · factorising & solving cubics
- Differential Calculus (5 lessons): limits & first principles · rules & tangents · cubic graphs · optimisation · rates of change

**Tools:** series lab (partial sums racing towards S∞), inverse lab (reflect in y = x, with domain restriction), annuity lab (savings and loans, with an amortisation table), polynomial lab (synthetic division), derivative lab (secant → tangent as h → 0), cubic lab (stationary points, concavity, inflection), optimisation lab (the open box).

**Practice:** 15 new question generators (sequences, sigma, inverses, logs, annuities, loans, remainder theorem, cubics, first principles, derivatives, tangents, stationary points, rates of change). 10 new Paper 1 exam questions.

**Engine:** the answer checker understands `log_b(x)` (change of base).

## Phase 4 – Grade 12 Geometry & Trigonometry ✅

**Content – Grade 12 (10 lessons):**

- Analytical Geometry (3 lessons): the equation of a circle · tangents to a circle · lines, circles & two circles
- Euclidean Geometry (3 lessons): ratio & the proportionality theorem · similar triangles · right triangles & Pythagoras by similarity – with the examinable proofs
- Trigonometry (4 lessons): compound angles (incl. the derivation of cos(α − β)) · double angles & identities · equations with compound & double angles · 2D & 3D problems

**Tools:** circle & tangent lab (any centre, drag P round the circle), proportion & similarity lab (slide a line parallel to a side; scale a triangle by k), compound & double-angle lab (the identity vs "the trap", as numbers and graphs), heights & distances lab (two angles of elevation in 2D; a tower on a plane in 3D that you can turn).

**Practice:** 10 new question generators (circle centre/radius, tangents, points on a circle, proportionality, similar triangles, exact compound values, double angles, simplifying, compound/double-angle equations, 2D & 3D heights). 7 new Paper 2 exam questions.

With Phase 4, the whole CAPS Grade 11 & 12 syllabus for Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry is live.

## Phase 5 – Exam season ✅

- **Mock papers** (`/exam/mock`): a full mock for Grade 11 and Grade 12, Paper 1 and Paper 2, built from the Exam Room questions, plus seeded **quick mocks** of about 50 marks. Timed exam mode (exam time at 1,2 min per mark, +25% extra time, or untimed), an optional hidden clock, a "pens down" alert, and the script saved as you write. After handing in, learners mark their own script against the memo and get their mark, percentage, **NSC achievement level (1–7)**, time used and a **breakdown by topic** with links to the right lessons. Papers and memos print cleanly.
- **Weak-spot coach** (on the Progress page): a mastery score for every topic from lesson quizzes, practice sets, self-marked exam questions, the latest mock of each paper and drill answers. It picks the three topics to work on next (weakest first) with the next lesson, the topic's exam questions and a **targeted drill** – or a mixed drill on all three.
- **Worksheet generator** (`/teachers/worksheets`): choose topics and 4–30 questions, print or save as PDF with answer space and a separate memo page with full working. The link reproduces the exact worksheet, so a class set and its memo always match.
- New badges: *Dress Rehearsal* (first mock) and *Distinction* (80%+ on a mock).
- Generated solutions now show each line of working as display maths.
