# FAQ & troubleshooting

**My progress disappeared.** Progress lives in the browser. Clearing browsing data, private/incognito mode or a different browser/device starts fresh. Use *Export progress* on the Progress page to back it up.

**The checker says my answer is wrong, but it's right!** Try: separating several answers with `;` or `or`; writing coordinates as `(2; -3)`; using `sqrt(…)` or `√`; not rounding unless the question asks. If it's still marked wrong, the "Show me how" button reveals the memo – and please tell your teacher so the question can be fixed.

**What can I type?** Numbers (`0,75` or `0.75`), fractions (`3/4`), surds (`2sqrt(5)`, `2√5`), powers (`x^2`), `π`, money (`R12 345,67`), sets (`x = 2 or x = -3`, `-1 ± √3`) and coordinates (`(2; -3)`). Tap the keyboard button for symbols.

**Why does it want "simplest surd form"?** Because the exam does: `√72` and `6√2` are equal, but only `6√2` gets full marks when the question says "simplify".

**Where is Grade 12 / Trigonometry / Geometry?** Coming in later phases – see *Build roadmap*. The Learn page shows each topic's phase.

**Is statistics or probability included?** Not at the moment – the app focuses on Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry.

**Animations make me dizzy.** Turn on *Calm mode* in Preferences (or your device's "reduce motion" setting).

**Can I use it offline?** Once a page has loaded it keeps working, but there is no full offline mode yet.
