# Overview

**MathsMzansi** is an interactive Grade 11 & 12 Mathematics companion for South African learners, built around the **CAPS** curriculum and the **NSC** exam format (Paper 1 and Paper 2). It covers the three strands **Algebra**, **Geometry** and **Trigonometry** – the whole syllabus except statistics and probability.

It is the sister app of **CodeMzansi** (IT) and **GeoMzansi** (Geography): same learning design, same accessibility features, same "no accounts, no tracking" promise.

## What's inside

- **Bite-sized lessons** – each split into 3–10 minute steps: a real-world hook, a live visual, worked examples revealed one step at a time, typed-answer practice, and a quick check.
- **Interactive maths tools** – a function grapher (lines, parabolas, hyperbolas, exponentials) with a *Graph Match* challenge, a quadratic lab, an inequality lab, a line-meets-parabola lab, a pattern machine, an exponent-law visualiser, a surd simplifier, a money lab, an investment timeline, a coordinate lab with draggable points, a circle-theorem explorer, a solid viewer, a CAST explorer, a trig graph lab and a triangle solver.
- **Endless practice** – question generators for every skill: a fresh problem every time, checked instantly, with the full worked solution.
- **Smart answer checking** – learners type real maths (`2sqrt(5)`, `x = 3 or x = -1`, `(2; -3)`, `2n^2 + n - 1`). The checker accepts any equivalent answer, gives specific feedback ("check your sign", "1 of 2 solutions", "leave it in surd form") and shows a live preview of what was typed.
- **Flashcards** with spaced repetition (Leitner boxes) for every key term and formula, plus a searchable glossary.
- **Quiz Arena** – mixed-topic rounds, relaxed or timed.
- **Exam Room** – NSC-style questions with marking memos (one tick per mark, method marks included) and exam technique.
- **Games** – Speed Solve, Graph Match, Formula Pairs, True/False Blitz, Odd One Out and Maths Hangman.
- **Progress** – XP, levels, streaks, badges, a 12-week activity map, and export/import.

## How it's being built

The full syllabus is a big project, so it ships in **phases** (see *Build roadmap*). Phase 1 delivered the whole platform plus **Grade 11 Algebra & Functions**; Phase 2 added **Grade 11 Geometry & Trigonometry**, so all of Grade 11 is live; Phase 3 adds **Grade 12 Algebra & Calculus** (sequences & series, inverses & logs, annuities, polynomials and differential calculus). Topics that aren't built yet appear on the Learn page as "Phase N – coming soon", so learners and teachers can see what's next.

## Who it's for

- **Learners** revising at home or catching up on a topic they missed.
- **Teachers** projecting the tools in class (the Toolkit page), setting a lesson as homework, or using the Exam Room for self-marked practice.
- **Every brain** – it's designed with ADHD and dyslexia in mind (see *ADHD & accessibility*).

> MathsMzansi is an independent study aid. Always check exam details with your teacher and the latest DBE examination guidelines.
